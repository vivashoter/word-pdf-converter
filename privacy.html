Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Your Privacy

ConvertDocGoose is designed to provide simple document conversion while minimizing the amount of personal information needed to use the service.

This Privacy Policy explains how information and documents are handled when you use ConvertDocGoose.

Uploaded Documents

Documents uploaded to ConvertDocGoose are used only to perform the document conversion requested by you.

Uploaded and converted documents are not permanently stored by ConvertDocGoose. Files are processed for the requested conversion and are not retained as permanent copies on our servers.

Google Drive

ConvertDocGoose may allow you to select documents from your Google Drive account.

When you choose to use this feature, Google may ask you to authorize ConvertDocGoose to access files that you select or otherwise authorize for use with the service.

ConvertDocGoose uses this access only to provide the document conversion functionality you request.

ConvertDocGoose does not use Google Drive access to browse unrelated files in your account or to permanently store copies of your Google Drive documents.

Google User Data

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing document conversion features of ConvertDocGoose.

ConvertDocGoose does not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising purposes.

Information We May Collect

ConvertDocGoose may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the website. This may include information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and general usage information.

If website analytics are enabled, analytics information may also be collected to help understand how visitors use ConvertDocGoose.

Cookies

ConvertDocGoose may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security, authentication, or analytics.

Third-Party Services

ConvertDocGoose may use third-party services necessary to operate the website and provide features such as hosting, Google authentication, Google Drive integration, and website analytics.

These services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through ConvertDocGoose. However, no internet service or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ConvertDocGoose changes or adds new features. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how ConvertDocGoose handles information, please contact us through the Contact page.
